The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at AVIT opened its five‑day International Faculty Development Programme with two sessions tracing the arc of modern artificial intelligence — from the architecture that made large language models possible, to the agentic systems now reshaping how education itself is delivered.

Day 1 — 7 September 2026

The Evolution of Modern AI

From Transformers and LLMs to RAG and Agentic Systems

Mr. Rupesh Kumar Prasad

Senior Manager, Technical Architect — Analog Devices, Inc., Wilmington, MA, United States

View LinkedIn profile

Mr. Prasad brings over 18 years of enterprise architecture and software engineering experience to the topic. At Analog Devices, where he has served as Technical Architect and Senior Manager since 2016, he leads architecture and delivery for large-scale systems spanning cloud, identity, data, IoT, and AI initiatives, with recent work architecting agentic AI solutions — autonomous, tool-using systems built with security and identity at their core. He is an IEEE Senior Member, a Raptors.dev Fellow, an SCRS Fellow, and an AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional. Speaking from that vantage point, he walked participants through how the field arrived at its current moment, and where the engineering patterns are headed next.

The Transformer as a turning point
How the attention mechanism replaced sequential architectures and unlocked the scale and parallelism behind today's foundation models.
From models to LLMs
How scaling laws, pretraining, and fine‑tuning turned transformers into general‑purpose systems capable of reasoning, summarization, and generation.
Grounding LLMs with RAG
Why retrieval‑augmented generation has become essential for connecting LLMs to real‑time, domain‑specific, and proprietary knowledge — reducing hallucination and improving factual reliability.
The shift to agentic systems
How LLMs are evolving from single‑turn responders into agents that break down goals, invoke tools, retrieve context, and take multi‑step actions.

Day 2 — 8 September 2026

From Generative AI to Agentic AI

The Next Generation of Intelligent Education

Official Day 2 flyer — Department of Computer Science and Engineering, AVIT, in association with the AI Nexus Club

Dr. Hema Durairaj

Senior Data Scientist — Publicis Sapient, Chennai

Dr. Durairaj holds a PhD in Machine Learning and Deep Learning for Computer Vision and works on digital business transformation at Publicis Sapient, where her projects span demand forecasting, smart pricing, and product development using applied AI/ML. She is a Microsoft Certified Data Scientist Associate and has held research fellowships with ICERM (USA) and CSIR (India). Building on Day 1's architectural foundation, her session moved the conversation from generative AI as a content tool to agentic AI as an active participant in how education is designed and delivered.

Generative AI in the classroom
Where large language models already help educators and learners — content creation, personalization, and adaptive assessment.
The leap from generating to acting
What separates a generative model that produces content from an agentic system that plans, decides, and carries out multi‑step tasks on a learner's behalf.
Agentic tutoring and learning assistants
How autonomous, tool‑using agents can personalize learning paths, track progress, and intervene proactively rather than waiting to be prompted.
A data science lens on reliability
Why sound data and feature engineering, model evaluation, and monitoring practices matter for building agentic education tools that are trustworthy at scale.
